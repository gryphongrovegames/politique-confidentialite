Privacy Policy – Minesy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Minesy, published by Gryphon Grove Games (sole proprietorship, France), developer of the application on the Google Play Store.

Summary

  • Your progress is saved on your device.
  • The game shows rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one. They are provided by Google AdMob.
  • Signing in to Google Play Games is optional.
  • For the daily bonus, the game asks a public web server for the time, without sending it any game data.
  • The game contains no analytics tool, creates no Minesy account, and we do not sell any data.

Data saved on your device

Minesy saves on your device the information the game needs: unlocked levels, stars and best scores, coins, themes bought with in-game coins, date of the last daily bonus and settings (sound, vibration, language, button layout). This information stays in the storage space of the application. It is not sent to Gryphon Grove Games, which runs no server for this game. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also saved in your Google account (see below).

Daily bonus and time check

To give the daily bonus only once a day, Minesy does not rely on the clock of your device: it asks a public web server for the current time (www.google.com, or www.cloudflare.com as a fallback). This request contains no game data. As with any visit to a website, this server receives the IP address of your device. Without an Internet connection, the daily bonus is not offered.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Minesy only shows rewarded ads: a video starts only if you tap a button made for it, for example to double a reward. The game contains no banner and no ad forced between two levels.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve them and measure their audience, the Google component included in the game may collect and process data such as the advertising ID of your device, your IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), information about the device (model, system, language), your interactions with the ads and diagnostic data. Depending on your choices, this data may be used to personalize ads.

To learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form provided by Google is shown at first launch, before any ad. You can change your choices at any time in the game: Settings › Ad choices.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings of your device.

Google Play Games

Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. If you sign in:

  • the game accesses your Google Play Games player ID and gamer name;
  • your total score is sent to the leaderboard and may be seen by other players, with your gamer name, according to your Google Play Games settings;
  • your progress (levels, stars, scores, coins, themes and date of the last daily bonus) is saved in your Google account through Google Play Games saved games, so that you can find it on another device. If you play on several devices, the progress is merged, keeping the best of each.

This data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy. Without signing in, the game remains fully playable.

What we do not do

Minesy includes no audience analytics tool. The game asks for no name, no e-mail address and no Minesy account. Gryphon Grove Games does not sell, rent or transfer any personal data.

Permissions

The game uses Internet access and the advertising ID of the device, which are needed for ads, Google Play Games and the time check of the daily bonus. It requests no access to your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Audience

Minesy is intended for people aged 13 and over. The game is not directed at children under 13.

Retention

Data saved on your device is kept until you reset your progress, clear the data of the application or uninstall it. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

Your rights and deleting your data

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have in particular a right of access, rectification, erasure and objection, and you can withdraw your consent at any time.

  • Game progress: in the game, Settings › Reset progress erases your progress on the device and, if you are signed in, replaces the save linked to your Google Play Games account with a blank progress. You can also clear the data of the application in the Android settings or uninstall it.
  • Leaderboard score and Google Play Games profile: resetting your progress does not erase your leaderboard score, as Google keeps the best score sent. The score and the profile are managed and deleted from the settings of Google Play Games and of your Google account.
  • Advertising: your choices can be changed in Settings › Ad choices; data held by Google is managed from your Google account.

For any question or request, write to us at the address below. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in France, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

Transfers outside the European Union

Google may process the data mentioned above on servers located outside the European Union, under the conditions described in its privacy policy.
