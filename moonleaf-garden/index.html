Privacy Policy – Moonleaf Garden

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Moonleaf Garden, published by Gryphon Grove Games (sole proprietorship, France), developer of the application on the Google Play Store.

Summary

  • Your garden is saved on your device.
  • The game shows rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one. They are provided by Google AdMob.
  • Google Play Games is optional; when you are signed in, a copy of your progress is kept in your Google account.
  • To protect timed rewards, the game asks a public web server for the time, without sending it any game data.
  • Reminder notifications are created on your device and can be turned off at any time.
  • The game contains no analytics tool, creates no Moonleaf Garden account, and we do not sell any data.

Data saved on your device

Moonleaf Garden saves on your device the information the game needs: gold, plants, familiars and their levels, aether seeds, season, letters received and read, tutorial, special order in progress, daily objectives, login calendar, magic rain and visits of the rich customer, with the time of the last save (used to compute the gold earned while you are away). It also keeps your settings (music, sound effects, vibrations, notifications, language) and the times of the reminder notifications already shown (to limit them to a few per day). This information stays in the storage space of the application. It is not sent to Gryphon Grove Games, which runs no server for this game. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also saved in your Google account (see below).

Time check

Gold earned while you are away, the login calendar, the daily objectives, special orders, the magic rain and the rich customer depend on the time. So that changing the clock of your device brings no advantage, Moonleaf Garden asks a public web server for the current time (www.google.com, or www.cloudflare.com as a fallback). This request contains no game data. As with any visit to a website, this server receives the IP address of your device. Without an Internet connection, the gold earned while away, the calendar, new daily objectives and the rich customer wait until the connection is back.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Moonleaf Garden only shows rewarded ads: a video starts only if you tap a button made for it (magic rain, double gold earned while away, rich customer, more time for a special order). The game contains no banner and no forced ad.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve them and measure their audience, the Google component included in the game may collect and process data such as the advertising ID of your device, your IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), information about the device (model, system, language), your interactions with the ads and diagnostic data. Depending on your choices, this data may be used to personalize ads.

On Android devices that support it, the Google component may also use the advertising features of Android (Privacy Sandbox: ad topics and ad attribution) under the same choices.

To learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form provided by Google is shown at first launch, before any ad. You can change your choices at any time in the game: Settings › Ad choices.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings of your device.

Google Play Games

Google Play Games is optional. At launch, the game uses the Google Play Games sign-in of your device if you already use it; you can also sign in from Settings. If you are signed in:

  • the game accesses your Google Play Games player ID and gamer name;
  • your progress (the garden data listed above, without your settings) is saved in your Google account through Google Play Games saved games, so that you can find it on another device or after reinstalling the game. If two saves differ, the most advanced one is kept.

The Google Play Games component may also collect diagnostic and usage data about the service (for example errors and performance). This data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy. Without signing in, the game remains fully playable.

Notifications

With your permission (asked once, at the end of the tutorial), Moonleaf Garden shows a few reminders, at most three per day and never at night: when your garden has produced for 8 hours, and when the daily gift is ready. These notifications are created and scheduled on your device only; no data is sent for them. You can turn them off in Settings › Notifications or in the Android settings of your device.

What we do not do

Moonleaf Garden includes no audience analytics tool. The game asks for no name, no e-mail address and no Moonleaf Garden account, and contains no in-app purchase. Gryphon Grove Games does not sell, rent or transfer any personal data.

Permissions

The game declares the following Android permissions:

  • Internet access and network state: needed for ads, Google Play Games and the time check;
  • advertising ID and Android advertising features (ad ID, ad topics, ad attribution): used by Google AdMob, according to your choices;
  • notifications and start at device boot: show the reminders and schedule them again after the device restarts;
  • vibration: vibrations of the game, which can be turned off in Settings;
  • technical permissions added by the Google components (keeping the device awake and running short background tasks, check the Google Play license, internal communication of the application): they give no access to your personal content.

The game requests no access to your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Audience

Moonleaf Garden is intended for people aged 13 and over. The game is not directed at children under 13.

Retention

Data saved on your device is kept until you clear the data of the application or uninstall it. The copy saved through Google Play Games is kept until you delete it from Google Play Games or your Google account. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

Your rights and deleting your data

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have in particular a right of access, rectification, erasure and objection, and you can withdraw your consent at any time.

  • Garden on the device: clear the data of the application in the Android settings, or uninstall it.
  • Copy in your Google account: delete the data of Moonleaf Garden from the settings of Google Play Games or of your Google account. Otherwise it is restored the next time you sign in.
  • Advertising: your choices can be changed in Settings › Ad choices; data held by Google is managed from your Google account.

A summary of these steps is available on the page Moonleaf Garden – Deleting your data.

For any question or request, write to us at the address below. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in France, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

Transfers outside the European Union

Google may process the data mentioned above on servers located outside the European Union, under the conditions described in its privacy policy.
